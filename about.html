The Director of Photography

Crafting the Visual Narrative

I am a cinematographer obsessed with how light, shadow, and movement convey emotion. My journey began not just behind a camera, but in the workshop—building rigs, modifying lenses, and understanding the mechanics of image capture from the ground up.

Recent feature film projects include Neon Nights and The Shadow Protocol, where I collaborated closely with directors to establish distinct visual languages. I believe that cinematography is problem-solving at its most creative: taking an abstract script and engineering a tangible, moving image.

Whether shooting on 35mm film or large format digital sensors, my philosophy remains constant: the technology must always serve the story.
